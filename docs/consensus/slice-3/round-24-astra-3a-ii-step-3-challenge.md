# Slice 3a-ii, step 3 design: Astra's review, round 1 of 3

Provenance: Astra (gpt-6.2-astra via Codex CLI, reasoning effort high), 2026-11-20, in reply to round-24-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design.md. Verbatim.

**The proposed release rule can strand the first correcting story, and the supersession rule gives contradictory instructions for a push already authorised.** Both are reachable through normal workflow operations. I would keep the two-part build, with the following amendments.

### Part (i) · Roadmaps, rejection and contracts in review

**S3-12.1 · BLOCKING · Reachable through story rejection, roadmap approval and `release_next`.** For the order `[rejected R, correction C, ordinary N]`, §2 requires C to be approved before C itself can be released. Placing C before R can avoid that particular deadlock, but the design permits both orders. There is a second deadlock regardless of placement: if C is rejected and D corrects C, approving D can never make C approved. Define rejection resolution transitively. The next correction in an unresolved chain may run despite the rejected ancestors it repairs. Ordinary successors wait until the chain ends in an approved correction. Keep each rejection immutable. Extend the test through **R rejected → C rejected → D approved → N released**, including a restart before D is released. [Proposed release rule](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-24-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design.md:36) · [Current release selector](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/domain/story.py:101)

**S3-12.2 · BLOCKING · Reachable through `Decisions.answer`, roadmap publication and approval.** A roadmap names the feature basis, but rewording a planned story is work on that story’s basis. Suppose a draft rewords S on feature basis 0. A story-scoped answer then advances S to basis 1 while S remains planned. Neither the feature basis nor the proposed state checks detect the change. Approval could apply obsolete wording and retire the very reconfirmation owed for that answer. Bind each reworded or dropped story to its identity, revision and story basis at authorship, and compare again at approval. An open question over S must also prevent applying a change to S. Test that a decision about S invalidates that edit while a decision about an untouched sibling leaves it approvable. [Roadmap approval effects](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-24-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design.md:28) · [Feature and story basis definitions](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/persistence/migrations/0011_basis.sql:4)

**S3-12.3 · BLOCKING · Reachable through a pending roadmap, a feature-wide answer and `reconfirm_roadmap`.** A proposal and its open approval gate have different lifetimes. Step 2 can supersede the gate while the proposal awaits reconfirmation. At that point §1’s “no other revision is waiting at a gate” can admit a third revision. Reopening the second gate also requires the *proposed* wording: its membership rows and the still-effective live story row cannot reconstruct an unapplied edit. Persist one outstanding proposal independently of its gate, including the exact proposed story versions or content. Define how it gets reconfirmed, replaced or sent back. Test R1 effective, R2 rewording an R1 story, a feature-wide answer, then restart. R1 must still expose the original text, and the next gate must show exactly one identifiable proposal. [Proposal rule](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-24-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design.md:22) · [Existing roadmap gate reconstruction](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/stories.py:617)

**S3-12.4 · BLOCKING for the promised recovery path · Reachable through `Decisions.answer` on a released story.** If an answer changes the acceptance criteria of the story holding the slot, the Analyst cannot truthfully reconfirm it. Section 1 forbids rewording the slot holder, and rejection is available only at a story gate that the stale story cannot reach. The feature therefore still requires cancellation. Give the human an audited way to return that active story for revision, preserving its integrated obligations, or explicitly retain this case as a Product Owner deferral. Test changing a criterion after one behaviour has integrated and before the story gate opens. [Protected story content](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-24-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design.md:24) · [Rejection entry point](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-24-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design.md:34)

**S3-12.5 · BLOCKING · Reachable through `Reviews.submit_review` and `Decisions.amend_contract`.** “Its reviews are withdrawn” needs a narrower meaning. Sentinel can already have raised a high finding while QA is still reviewing. Amending the contract must supersede the cycle, retire its tasks and invalidate its approvals, while preserving the finding and its issuing role’s authority to dispose of it. Test that a late QA submission changes nothing and that an empty Sentinel submission on the successor candidate cannot clear the carried finding. [Proposed amendment](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-24-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design.md:57) · [Existing finding authority](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/reviews.py:619)

### Part (ii) · Obligations and supersession

**S3-12.6 · BLOCKING · Reachable through supersession approval, `Integration.integrate`, `Decisions.replace` and reconciliation.** Authorise Z’s push with X exempted, lose the reply, then change the decision basis. Section 3 voids the authority immediately, but the outstanding intent still owns a push that may already have broken X. Looking up the *currently standing* authority at settlement can leave X active after Z lands, or allow a second replacement of X while the first remains uncertain. Bind the exact exemption and replacement obligation to the intent at authorisation. Reserve X until that intent settles. A later decision prevents new use of the authority. Convergence consumes the recorded exemption exactly once; definitive failure leaves X active. Test both outcomes with the basis change **between** authorisation and recovery. [Supersession lifetime](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-24-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design.md:44) · [Intent reconciliation](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/integration.py:791)

**S3-12.7 · BLOCKING · Reachable through `Assignments.claim` after a decision during an outstanding push.** Section 4 says the Verifier completes its reconfirmation by declaring the behaviour to be replaced while the intent is outstanding. Step 2 deliberately makes that grant unclaimable. Worse, if the remote ultimately refuses the push, the behaviour never contributed an obligation that a supersession could replace. Keep the assignment wanted until settlement. On convergence, permit declaration against the actual obligation ID. On definitive non-integration, provide the route back to contract amendment without requiring an obligation. Test both paths, with a fresh Verifier claiming after settlement. [Declaration rule](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-24-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design.md:58) · [Current claim guard](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/assignments.py:334)

**S3-12.8 · BLOCKING · Reachable through `Stories.seal`, `Stories.assess` and story approval.** Section 5 changes the currency rule for sealing alone. After Z replaces X, sealing can succeed while assessment still refuses X’s stale contract. Simply excluding X everywhere would create the opposite defect: X might have been the only behaviour demonstrating a criterion, while Z demonstrates a different one. Define which integrated evidence now demonstrates each criterion. Use that same mapping for sealing, assessment and the immutable gate manifest, with X retained as history. Extend the §6 test through **human approval**, and include a case where Z leaves one criterion unsupported. [New seal rule](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-24-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design.md:72) · [Assessment entry point](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/stories.py:247) · [Gate manifest](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/stories.py:926)

**S3-12.9 · BLOCKING · Reachable through supersession approval and `Features.close(cancel)`.** Approve X→Z, then cancel Z’s feature before an intent exists. Cancellation need not change any field in the listed approval basis. Z can never integrate, yet its standing authority can prevent another feature from replacing X. Require X to be an active behaviour obligation in Z’s project and Z to be capable of a future integration. Void unused authority when Z is cancelled or abandoned, leaving X active. If an intent already owns the exemption, apply S3-12.6. Test that a second feature can obtain authority after cancellation and that X remains in its regression set until that new authority is granted. [Approval basis and uniqueness](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-24-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design.md:43)

**S3-12.10 · NON-BLOCKING · Reachable through `Integration.require_check`.** Section 7 says existing project checks are changed through `require_check`. The current operation is add-only and refuses a duplicate check name. Document that limitation for this slice. The independent project check in the equal-digest test should continue to block Z; treating its re-registration as replacement would weaken that test. [Scope statement](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-24-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design.md:93) · [Existing operation](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/integration.py:124)
