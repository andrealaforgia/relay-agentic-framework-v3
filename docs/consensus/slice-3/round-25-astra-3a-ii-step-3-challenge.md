# Slice 3a-ii, step 3 design: Astra's review, round 2 of 3

Provenance: Astra (gpt-6.2-astra via Codex CLI, reasoning effort high), 2026-11-20, in reply to round-25-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design-v2.md. Verbatim.

**Part (i) is not ready to build as written.** The recursive definition of “resolved” handles a correction that is itself rejected. The release rule still allows both a deadlock and an ordinary story escaping the correction gate, depending only on where the Analyst places it.

### Part (i) · Changes needed before building

**S3-13.1 · BLOCKING · Reachable through roadmap approval and `release_next`.** Both `[R rejected, N ordinary, C corrects R]` and `[N, R, C]` satisfy the proposal rules. In the first order, N waits for R and C waits for N. In the second, N has no unresolved predecessor and can start immediately. Make an unresolved rejection a feature-wide prerequisite for ordinary work. While a chain is unresolved, select its eligible correcting story independently of ordinary positions, or reject orders that cannot meet that rule. Test both orders, including release after restart. [Proposal validation and release rules](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-25-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design-v2.md:23)

**S3-13.2 · BLOCKING · Reachable through a second roadmap approval.** The exactly-one check looks at whether R has a correction in the **old** effective revision. Once that revision contains planned C, the next proposal can drop C or keep C and add a second correction of R. The condition no longer applies. Validate the resulting membership: every unresolved rejected story must have exactly one direct correction there. Preserve historical correction links. Test dropping the sole planned correction and adding a duplicate. [Correction membership rule](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-25-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design-v2.md:28)

**S3-13.3 · BLOCKING · Reachable through delayed `publish_roadmap`.** Naming the outstanding proposal does not identify the effective roadmap the Analyst read. An Analyst drafts a reorder against R1 with no proposal outstanding. Another proposal is published and approved as R2. The delayed draft arrives with `none`, still the correct feature decision basis, and unchanged story revisions. It can silently put the old order back before the human as a fresh proposal. Require publication to name **both** the expected effective revision and the expected outstanding proposal, and compare both under lock. Test this with two order-only edits so story revision checks cannot mask the defect. [Publication preconditions](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-25-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design-v2.md:21)

**S3-13.4 · BLOCKING · Reachable through a feature-wide answer, roadmap reconfirmation and `request_changes`.** R2 proposes rewording S on basis 0. A feature-wide answer moves S to basis 1. Reconfirming the same stored R2 opens a gate whose approval must fail because the edit still names basis 0. Separately, if R2 is sent back, R1 remains effective but stale; completing R2’s reconfirmation must not consume the only work owed for R1. Give the effective revision and the proposal distinct reconfirmation targets. If an edit’s basis changed, require a successor proposal or an explicit new attestation for that exact edit before reopening an approvable gate. Extend the existing restart test through approval, and test the sent-back branch through release under R1. [Edit and reconfirmation rules](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-25-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design-v2.md:33) · [Current roadmap assignment creation](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/decisions.py:683)

**S3-13.5 · BLOCKING · Reachable through active-story rejection.** Gate rejection already has an immutable target. Direct rejection names a story whose revision can change while the human’s request is delayed. A rejection prepared for revision 1 can arrive after a gate `request_changes` has put revision 2 back into work. Both satisfy “being worked on”, yet rejection is permanent. Bind direct rejection to the project, story, revision and decision basis shown to the human. Under the exclusive gate lock, return a stale receipt on mismatch and the original receipt on exact replay. Test that a revision-1 request leaves revision 2 and its assignments untouched. [Active rejection rule](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-25-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design-v2.md:46) · [Current story revision transition](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/stories.py:848)

**S3-13.6 · NON-BLOCKING · Reachable through withdrawal followed by `Decisions.answer`.** Dropping S retires its current reconfirmation, but a subsequent feature-wide answer can owe another one: step 2 excludes approved, rejected and cancelled stories from that scan, and `withdrawn` is new. Define withdrawn and rejected stories as historical for new story-scoped questions and reconfirmations. Test that a later feature-wide answer creates no inbox item or assignment for the withdrawn story. [Withdrawal effects](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-25-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design-v2.md:34) · [Current scope query](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/decisions.py:660)

### Part (ii) · Can wait for its design round

**S3-13.7 · BLOCKING for part (ii) · Reachable through supersession proposal, ordinary integration and late approval.** Z can pass X and integrate normally while its supersession proposal waits. That changes none of the four listed basis fields. A late approval can therefore reserve the only standing authority over X for a Z that will never integrate again. At approval recheck that X is active and available, Z is still eligible, and no already-authorised intent for Z fixes a different set of exemptions. Test approval both after ordinary integration and while that ordinary push is outstanding. [Proposal eligibility and approval basis](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-25-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design-v2.md:78)

**S3-13.8 · BLOCKING for part (ii) · Reachable through two supersession approvals and `Integration.integrate`.** One authority per X still permits `X1 → Z` and `X2 → Z`. The regression rule can omit both, while the intent is specified to record and retire one. Choose the cardinality explicitly. The smaller implementation enforces one standing or reserved authority per Z as well as per X and refuses the second approval. If multiple replacements are required, reserve, persist and settle the complete set atomically. Test two distinct source obligations targeting one Z. [Authority and intent cardinality](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-25-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design-v2.md:82)

**S3-13.9 · BLOCKING for the refusal branch · Reachable through a remote hook refusal and `amend_contract`.** Section 6 says a refused push puts the behaviour back in review. The existing settlement parks it in `blocked`; returning to review requires a separate authorised retry, and that retry also requires the cycle still to be satisfied. Preserve the human retry policy. Specify the audited path from a definitively refused intent to amendment, including what happens if a recorded ref conflict has also blocked the cycle. The acceptance test should start with a real hook refusal and assert the intermediate state. [Proposed refusal path](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-25-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design-v2.md:95) · [Actual refusal settlement](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/integration.py:1284) · [Retry preconditions](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/integration.py:266)

**S3-13.10 · BLOCKING for part (ii) · Reachable through `Stories.seal`, `Stories.assess` and integration settlement.** A story can be sealed and assessed while Z is still being built. Suppose X and Z both demonstrate the same criterion. Z’s integration retires X. Coverage still passes, but the manifest now shows only Z in force, while the Verifier and Analyst attested to X and Z. Bind the seal to the version of the criterion-to-behaviour mapping it attests to. When retirement changes that mapping, require a fresh seal and assessment before opening the gate. Test retirement **after** assessment, with the criterion still covered; an unsupported-criterion test cannot expose this history. [Shared evidence rule](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-25-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design-v2.md:99) · [Existing seal contract](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/stories.py:207) · [Gate evaluation](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/stories.py:515)
