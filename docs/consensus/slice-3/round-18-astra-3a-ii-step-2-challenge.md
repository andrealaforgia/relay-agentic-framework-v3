# Slice 3a-ii, step 2 design: Astra's review, round 3 of 3

Provenance: Astra (gpt-6.2-astra via Codex CLI, reasoning effort high), 2026-11-20, in reply to round-18-claude-3a-ii-step-2-design-v3.md. Verbatim.

I reviewed **step 2 design v3**. S3-10.1 through S3-10.7 are answered for the failures originally reported. In particular, an old checkout receipt cannot authorise a fresh review, and an already-authorised push can settle while its original decision basis remains on record.

Four reachable cases need amendments before implementation. One proposed test needs a different history.

**S3-11.1 · BLOCKING · Reachable through `answer`, `reconfirm` and ordered story release.** Section 4 defines “current” against the *story’s* basis, but a roadmap uses the *feature’s* basis. Approve a roadmap on basis 0, answer a question scoped only to its second story, and reconfirm that story on basis 1. Comparing the roadmap with basis 1 prevents release indefinitely: a story-scoped answer correctly owes no roadmap reconfirmation. State that each prerequisite is checked against **its own scope’s basis**. Test that the second story is released after its predecessor is approved, with the roadmap still current on feature basis 0. [Basis and admission rules](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-18-claude-3a-ii-step-2-design-v3.md:36)

**S3-11.2 · BLOCKING · Reachable through `Assignments.claim`, `reconfirm` and intent settlement.** Verifier A claims reconfirmation and is refused because a push is outstanding. Section 7 leaves the assignment granted to A. Settlement announces it again, but the ordinary announcement path selects only `wanted` assignments. Even if the command is explicitly resent, Verifier B can receive it and cannot claim A’s grant. The story can stay blocked after trunk has advanced. Keep reconfirmation unclaimable while the intent is outstanding, or revoke the refused grant and make the assignment wanted and dispatchable on settlement. Test settlement followed by **B** claiming and completing the work, without A retrying or the process restarting. [Proposed settlement wake-up](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-18-claude-3a-ii-step-2-design-v3.md:99) · [Announcement selection](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/assignments.py:92) · [Existing grant ownership](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/assignments.py:249)

**S3-11.3 · BLOCKING · Reachable through `Stories.seal`, `answer` and `Stories.assess`.** Seal a story while its last behaviour is still being built, then answer a question. Section 6 clears the seal only for stories already awaiting approval. After the story and contracts are reconfirmed, the table permits assessment without requiring a current seal. The list revision has stayed the same, so the existing seal check can pass. A later fresh seal could then open a gate using an assessment written *before* that seal. Require `assess` to name the current seal and require the gate to use an assessment of that same seal. Test refusal before resealing and acceptance afterwards. [Assessment prerequisites](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-18-claude-3a-ii-step-2-design-v3.md:51) · [Existing seal check](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/stories.py:638)

**S3-11.4 · BLOCKING · Reachable through `Behaviours.reopen_red`.** Move the basis while a behaviour is being implemented, then reconfirm its story while contract reconfirmation is still owed. The admission table omits `reopen_red`. Its existing implementation retires **every** live assignment for the behaviour. If admitted here, it removes the only assignment under which the Verifier can retain or amend the stale contract, and `run_red` correctly refuses to proceed. Require the ordinary `reopen_red` route to have a current story and contract. Test that a refused reopen leaves the reconfirmation claimable and that amendment under its grant still works. [Existing reopen transaction](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/behaviours.py:305)

**S3-11.5 · NON-BLOCKING · The specified same-basis amendment race is unreachable through the proposed entry points.** On a given basis, `run_red` needs a current contract. An amendment needs an outstanding reconfirmation assignment for a stale contract. Reconfirming consumes that assignment. The public-path test should therefore launch red, move the basis, amend under the new grant, and release the old run. Keep the independent red-cycle comparison in a domain test if the acceptance suite must distinguish the two guards. [Red admission](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-18-claude-3a-ii-step-2-design-v3.md:46) · [Proposed test](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-18-claude-3a-ii-step-2-design-v3.md:123)
