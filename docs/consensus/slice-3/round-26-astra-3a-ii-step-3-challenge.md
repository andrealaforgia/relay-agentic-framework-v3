### Part (i)

**S3-14.1 · BLOCKING · Reachable through roadmap publication and approval.** Reject R, approve its correction C, then publish a proposal adding D as another correction of R. Section 1 permits a new story to correct a rejected story, but enforces exactly one correction only while R is *unresolved*. R is already resolved through C. The proposal can therefore give R two direct corrections, although the release rule depends on “the story that corrects it”. Require at most one direct correction of any rejected story in the resulting membership, and exactly one for each unresolved rejection when approving a revision. Test that adding D after C’s approval is refused and the effective revision stays unchanged. [Proposal validation](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-26-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design-v3.md:23)

**S3-14.2 · BLOCKING · Reachable through `Decisions.amend_contract`, fresh reviews and `Integration.integrate`.** A remote hook refuses a push. A subsequent decision owes the Verifier a reconfirmation. The existing amendment operation checks that `replaces` identifies the current contract and accepts the identical contract as its replacement. Extending that operation to `blocked` gives the Verifier a route back to red with nothing amended. With trunk unchanged, the same baseline and candidate can pass fresh checks and reach another push without the Product Owner’s retry. For a parked behaviour, require a first effective amendment to change the contract digest. Test an identical amendment after a real refusal. It must leave the behaviour parked, the grant available and the refused intent untouched. A genuinely changed contract can take the fresh-red route already specified. Also align section 6’s “back in review” wording with section 3’s `blocked` outcome. [Refused-push rule](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-26-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design-v3.md:75) · [Existing amendment check](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/decisions.py:515)

**S3-14.3 · NON-BLOCKING · Reachable through `Decisions.ask` and story release.** Keep one acceptance test for the inherited release guards on the new correction branch. Let another feature hold the project slot, approve C as R’s correction, open a question over C, then free the slot. Neither C nor an ordinary sibling may start. After the answer and required reconfirmation, C starts once. The two ordering tests in v3 would both pass if the new branch selected C solely by its correction link. The existing release operation already computes whether each story is stopped. [Correction selection](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-26-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design-v3.md:60) · [Existing release guards](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/stories.py:657)

### Part (ii)

**S3-14.4 · BLOCKING for part (ii) · Reachable through supersession proposal and human gate decision.** The Verifier proposes X1 → Z. The Product Owner sends it back because X2 is the obligation that should be replaced. Section 5 bars a second proposal for Z, but defines how to void an *approved authority* only. It gives no transition that ends the first proposal and frees Z. Give a pending proposal a durable terminal outcome on human refusal, withdrawal or invalidation. Close its gate and inbox item in the same transaction. Test that X2 → Z can then be approved, a delayed approval for X1 → Z is stale, and X1 remains active throughout. [Supersession eligibility and lifecycle](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-26-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design-v3.md:87)

**S3-14.5 · BLOCKING for part (ii) · Reachable through integration settlement after story approval.** Approve story A with X as its sole demonstration of a criterion. In a later story B, integrate Z under authority to replace X. The retirement changes what is in force today. Section 6 directs settlement to clear the seal of a story whose mapping changes, which would clear A’s seal even though A is historical and `Stories.seal` accepts only a story being worked on. Limit resealing to live stories whose own mapping changed. Preserve A’s original seal, assessment, manifest and approval, and show the subsequent retirement as history. Test across two stories and reread A’s approval after restart. The same-story resealing test cannot establish this. [Retirement and seals](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-26-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design-v3.md:112) · [Seal eligibility](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/stories.py:197)

**S3-14.6 · NON-BLOCKING · Reachable through a decision during an outstanding push and subsequent refusal settlement.** The negative-settlement test checks that X remains active. That also holds when a standing authority still exempts Z, so the assertion cannot detect an obsolete exemption being restored. Exercise both outcomes after a definitive refusal. With the basis unchanged, the authority may stand again. With the basis changed during the push, a later Z batch must include X and another eligible replacement must be able to obtain authority over X. V3 already states the intended result. This test distinguishes the two states after recovery. [Reservation and settlement](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-26-claude-3a-ii-step-3-design-v3.md:95)
