# Astra's review of the slice 3b design (v1)

Provenance: written by Astra (gpt-6.2-astra via Codex) on 2026-11-21 in reply to `round-41-claude-3b-design.md`; copied verbatim below.

Step 1 needs amendments before it is a sound build contract. A duplicate delivery can authorise another paid call after the first result is durable, startup can invalidate the grant needed to apply a known result, and the proposed callback cannot make Git, the oracle and PostgreSQL one transaction. There is also a separate step 3 gap on the ordinary path from a satisfied review to the first integration.

I checked the application paths at `fa83d49` and the installed Claude Code 2.1.330 help. The reachability judgements below concern the proposed worker entry points composed with the existing services. No paid provider call was made.

**S3-18.1 · BLOCKING · Step 1 · Reachable through worker redelivery.** The unique index stops protecting an assignment as soon as its invocation becomes `returned`. Deliver the command again after recording the reply and before applying it. The same worker can obtain the same grant, and a second intent satisfies the index. Likewise, `set_aside` falls outside the index even though the person chose to keep the work parked. Require one admission decision under the assignment lock. A known unapplied result goes to Apply, a parked assignment stays parked, and an authorised retry consumes its permission once. Acknowledging Redis after Record also needs a live consumer for unapplied results. Test a duplicate on both sides of acknowledgement, with the process still running, and require one provider call and one effect. [Assignment claim](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/assignments.py:326)

**S3-18.2 · BLOCKING · Step 1 · Reachable through worker or control-plane restart.** Starting QA does not establish that a Builder process with an `intended` call has died. A full control-plane start has the opposite problem. It revokes every grant, so a review recorded as `returned` just before the crash can no longer submit against its original checkout. Give startup recovery one owner that can establish which local processes have ended. Specify how it applies a still-valid known result once, preserving its original grant, checkout and basis as evidence, and how it records a result overtaken by cancellation or replacement. Test a second role starting during a live call and a full restart after Record. I agree with leaving Claude’s private transcript out of recovery. A genuinely unknown call stays parked for a human under D-6. [Startup](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/features.py:81) · [Review receipt and grant](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/reviews.py:535) · [Recovery requirement](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/SPEC.md:418)

**S3-18.3 · BLOCKING · Step 1 · Reachable through `prepare_review`, pause and invocation admission.** A review checkout is already a first effect. Pause deliberately leaves that grant in place so a review in flight may finish. If pause commits after checkout but before Intend, section 4’s current-grant test can admit a fresh paid call during the pause. Define when Relay admits the call under the project gate lock, check every applicable stop there, and apply that rule to each paid retry. Record the task, grant and context versions admitted. Test both commit orders, including a malformed first reply followed by a pause before retry. [Review first effect](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/assignments.py:174)

**S3-18.4 · BLOCKING · Step 1 contract, exercised in steps 2 and 3 · Reachable through Builder and Verifier Apply.** `act_once` protects writes in its own database transaction. A Builder can publish its attempt and die before `run_green`. A Verifier can commit `propose` and die before registering the contract. Replaying the whole callback can then reject work that partly succeeded. Define the durable outcome of Apply for database-only operations and for operations with several effects. The latter need recorded progress keyed to the invocation, so recovery resumes at the first unfinished effect. For a build, retain the exact candidate object before disposing of the workspace, bind it to one attempt ref, and reconcile that ref before starting green. For `specify`, include the required `begin_test_design` transition. Test a crash after the accepted push and another after the behaviour proposal. Neither recovery should call the model again. [Receipt boundary](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/persistence/receipts.py:10) · [Green execution](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/behaviours.py:286) · [Contract registration precondition](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/domain/behaviour.py:63)

**S3-18.5 · BLOCKING · Step 1 adapter · Reachable through ordinary `invoke`.** A terminal Claude result can report a structured-output error and usage. A successful result carries its schema output separately from its prose. Preserve the terminal envelope, classify its success or error, validate the successful `structured_output`, and retain reported usage for both. A known terminal error must never apply a workflow reply. Also pass the requested `--effort` or refuse it. The installed CLI advertises that flag and the proposed command omits it, so the ledger could claim an effort Relay never requested. Use recorded fixtures for success, terminal error and a stream ending without a terminal event. [Claude structured-output contract](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-sdk/structured-outputs)

**S3-18.6 · BLOCKING · Step 2, before any tool-enabled real invocation · Reachable through Builder, QA and Sentinel.** I accept separate review exports bound to the same pinned commit. The remaining boundary is the process that reads them. The installed canonical QA definition requests `Bash` and named skills. A working directory, a clone without a remote and an environment with selected variables removed do not establish what that process can read or execute. Specify an enforced role-specific filesystem, process and network boundary, a trusted source for the effective agent definition and skills, and a confined way for the Builder to create its local commit. Keep each reviewer’s original checkout receipt with its invocation. Test with a harmless marker outside the workspace and a candidate-local agent definition that would otherwise change the review. **D-5 may remain deferred only if this boundary keeps capabilities and command payloads inside the trusted supervisor.** Same-host deployment alone does not establish that. [Security requirements](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/SPEC.md:400) · [D-5](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/DECISIONS.md:26)

**S3-18.7 · BLOCKING · Step 3 · Reachable on the first successful behaviour.** Accepted green creates a pending candidate. Publication is driven by the reconciliation pass. Once both reviews land, `_apply_gate` records `review.gate_satisfied`, but it owes no first integration. The existing `continue_integration` consumes work owed for an earlier batch. Name the durable control-plane trigger for candidate publication and for the **first** integration of a newly satisfied cycle, including recovery if the process stops after either commit. The feature test should reach trunk with only workers and human gate decisions. A fixture calling `publish_candidates`, `integrate` or a manual pass would conceal the gap. [Review settlement](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/reviews.py:696) · [Reconciliation pass](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/features.py:115) · [Initial integration and successor](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/integration.py:189)

**S3-18.8 · BLOCKING · Step 3 · Reachable through `request_changes`, `revise` and `reconfirm`.** The four new triggers cover first delivery but leave existing routes without a worker. Sending a story back clears its seal and starts a new revision without another release or integration. `Assignments.claim` expressly refuses `revise`, yet every task in the design starts by claiming. A Verifier saying a contract no longer stands also needs a route to `amend_contract` or `declare_replaced`. Define, per task, which transition owes it, which reply applies it, and which event finally discharges it. Roadmap publication and human approval are separate events. Test a sent-back story and a sent-back repair proposal through workers after restart. [Claim rules](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/assignments.py:346) · [Existing Verifier routes](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/decisions.py:503)

**S3-18.9 · BLOCKING · Step 3 · Reachable from any worker task.** A question or assumption is a valid reply, but neither delivers the artefact that prompted the call. Marking the assignment done loses that work. Immediately calling again after an assumption can spend repeatedly without advancing it. Record the question or assumption once under the invocation, preserve the original obligation, and specify who may resume it, on which basis and under which grant. Test a Verifier asking instead of supplying a contract, a restart, a human answer, and exactly one resumed task using the new basis. Give an assumption-only reply an explicit bounded outcome too.

**S3-18.10 · BLOCKING · Step 1 usage record, step 4 summary · Reachable through an ordinary paid call or a late result.** Four aggregate counters cannot determine an estimate when cache writes have different prices by lifetime and the provider omits the split. Preserve the reported model and available breakdown, keep observed provider cost separate, and mark an estimate unknown wherever its inputs are missing. Also define the summary’s cutoff. A story can be rejected with a Builder call in flight and its correction delivered before that call reports usage. The immutable summary should disclose that outstanding call as unknown at completion. The live costs query can subsequently show the observation. Test both histories. [PO-9](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/DECISIONS.md:16)

The step 2 workspace and step 3 scheduling findings can wait for their respective builds. The invocation rules and usage record need agreement before step 1 because those are the records every later step must recover from.
