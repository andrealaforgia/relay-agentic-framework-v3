**3C-S3-1.1. The live account loses spend by role and charges the provider attributed to a model. Reachable through `relay costs` and `relay watch --tokens`.** If Builder and QA use the same model, the view gives their call counts, their combined tokens and one provider charge. It cannot tell the Product Owner what either role used or cost. PO-8 explicitly asks for token spend per role. There is also a real source for model-level charges: the adapter reads `modelUsage.costUSD`, including in the recorded CLI fixture. The invocation keeps the whole envelope, but its usage projection drops that field. Test two roles sharing a model and one call billing a second model. Each role should show its own usage, observed charges and estimates by table. Show a model charge when the provider supplied it, and count the whole call charge once. The same account should be selectable for one feature in the following token view. **3C-S3-1.3. The board can show an open question as having waited zero seconds indefinitely. Reachable through a worker asking and `relay watch`.** The status query includes an `asked` assignment while its question is open. The age query includes only `wanted` and `granted`. The join then defaults the missing age to zero. It also keys ages by `(kind, subject)`, which both reviewers share. A fresh QA successor and an older Sentinel review of the same behaviour can therefore display the same age. Read age alongside the assignment it belongs to, using its identity. A question left open across two polls and two reviews owed at different times cover both failures. **3C-S3-1.4. The board omits who holds the grant and what awaits the Product Owner. Reachable through ordinary claims and gate decisions.** With two live Builders and one granted build, the board says `builder 2` and `taken up`. If the holder exits before recovery, `builder 1` gives the person no way to tell whether the remaining Builder holds that work. Similarly, an approval and a parked paid retry both display as `waiting for the Product Owner: 1`. Show the grant holder with its liveness, distinguish time owed from time held, and give each waiting item its kind and subject or inbox handle. The full evidence can stay in `relay show`.
