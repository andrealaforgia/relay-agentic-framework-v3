# Slice 3a-ii, step 4, design: Astra's first review

Provenance: reply of Astra (gpt-6.2-astra via Codex) on 2026-11-21 to `round-31-claude-3a-ii-step-4-design.md`, copied verbatim.

**S3-4.10 is partly answered.** The proposed batch identifies the roadmap, stories, obligations and Git commit it checks. Two guarantees still need work before implementation: the final trunk read must account for a push already in flight, and a human request for changes must survive the next readiness evaluation.

**S3-15.1 · BLOCKING · An authorised push can land after the final trunk read.** Reachable through `Integration.integrate` and completion settlement. Another feature’s push can pass `count_the_send`, release its database lock, and wait at the remote. Completion then takes the exclusive gate lock and reads `H`. Git can accept the push before the summary commits. There is also a reverse gap: Git can already show the new head while PostgreSQL still holds the old obligations because integration settlement has not committed. Under the existing sole-writer assumption, require **no outstanding integration intent for the project** before the final bounded read, and hold the exclusive gate lock from that check through summary commit. An outstanding intent or unreadable head defers completion. Test both sides of remote acceptance, including a push that retires an obligation. [Push authorisation and send](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/integration.py:691) · [Obligation settlement](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/integration.py:877)

**S3-15.2 · BLOCKING · `request_changes` leaves the feature immediately ready to complete again.** Reachable through `Stories.decide` followed by `Features.start`. The failed check’s gate returns the feature to `delivering`, but its roadmap is still current, every story is resolved and no question is open. A restart can put it straight back into `checking`, where the Analyst cannot publish the requested revision. Persist that a roadmap change is owed, tied to the failed batch and effective revision. Keep completion ineligible until a successor revision answering that request is approved. Test a restart immediately after `request_changes` and again after a proposed repair is sent back. An ordinary repair story must be admissible when every earlier story was approved and there is no rejected story to name as a correction target.

**S3-15.3 · BLOCKING · Generation identity is recorded but does not fence settlement.** Reachable through completion take-up and restart recovery. Section 3 never requires the settling batch to be `running` and the current generation. A late failure from generation 1 can otherwise open a gate after generation 2 has completed the feature on the same `H`, roadmap and obligations. Under the lock, let only the current running generation decide. Enforce one live completion request per feature, one successor per interrupted generation, and one summary and outbox notice per completion. Test an old failure arriving after a newer success and the reverse ordering. Matching snapshot fields alone cannot distinguish those calls.

**S3-15.4 · BLOCKING · Readiness can change without waking the control plane.** Reachable through `Stories.decide` at a roadmap gate. Let the last story be approved while a proposal is outstanding. Sending that proposal back then makes the effective roadmap ready, without approving either a story or a roadmap in that transaction. Include this transition. Also name the post-commit caller that actually consumes `complete` after the ordinary last-story approval. `Stories.decide` currently returns its receipt, and the pass runs on explicit service paths. A durable assignment alone cannot meet “with nothing else called”. Test both routes through the public action, including a lost reply and exact replay. [Roadmap sent back](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/stories.py:1244) · [Current reconciliation entry point](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/features.py:53)

**S3-15.5 · BLOCKING · An unavailable repository has no unambiguous recovery owner.** Reachable through final settlement with a timed-out trunk read. Section 3 leaves the batch `running` for the next pass; section 6 interrupts running batches on **start**. An ordinary pass must be able to recover this feature without interrupting another batch whose oracle is genuinely running. Give the failed read a durable retryable outcome, or a distinct state awaiting trunk verification, with one owed follow-up. Also specify when execution receipts commit: recording them only in settlement loses every finished observation if the process dies just before that transaction. Test a successful first check, interruption before settlement, and a failed head read followed by ordinary reconciliation without a process restart.

**S3-15.6 · BLOCKING · A decision at a failed completion gate has no specified basis or invalidation rule.** Reachable through `Decisions.ask`, `Decisions.answer` and `Stories.decide`. A feature-wide answer while `needs_decision` makes the roadmap stale. The current invalidation query closes story gates in scope and singles out `roadmap` for feature-level gates. A `completion` gate with no story falls through it. Bind the gate to the failed batch and its evidence manifest. A feature decision must supersede that gate and define how reconfirmation returns the feature to checking. Conversely, `retry` after another feature moves trunk should remain meaningful: the human is requesting fresh checks on the new head. Test both histories, including replay of a decision sent to the old gate. [Existing gate invalidation](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/decisions.py:761)

**S3-15.7 · BLOCKING · A completed feature can still acquire new decision context.** Reachable through `Decisions.assume` or replacement of a feature-wide decision. The terminal examples cover questions and proposals, but a worker assumption can change the live context after the summary freezes it, and replacing a feature-wide decision can owe a roadmap reconfirmation. Apply the terminal check to both paths. An exact replay of a previously effective human request must still return its original receipt. Test all three after completion and assert unchanged context, assignments and summary. [Assumption entry point](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/decisions.py:322) · [Replacement guard](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/decisions.py:895)

**S3-15.8 · NON-BLOCKING · The summary needs a rule for deciding which findings remain open limitations.** Reachable through `Reviews.submit_review` followed by `Features.summary_of`. A finding can retain status `open` after its issuing role verified the fix on the integrated candidate. Filtering on status would report a repaired defect as a delivered limitation. Derive the list from the applicable integrated review cycle, its frozen policy, withdrawals and per-cycle verifications. Test an unresolved low finding beside one verified fixed on the delivered candidate; only the former belongs in open limitations. [Review gate semantics](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/domain/review.py:81)

I accept project-wide active obligations as this slice’s feature checks, `unknown` for unmeasured usage, and a completion notice without another approval gate. The notice and immutable summary should commit together. This was a design review against the existing entry points; there is no step 4 implementation to test yet.
