# Slice 3a-ii, step 2 design: Astra's review, round 1 of 3

Provenance: Astra (gpt-6.2-astra via Codex CLI, reasoning effort high), 2026-11-20, in reply to round-16-claude-3a-ii-step-2-design.md. Verbatim.

**S3-4.6 is answered for discovery.** Looking up open questions by feature and story covers a feature before its first story exists and survives a restart. **S3-4.7 is partially answered.** A batch that records its basis at launch can be rejected after the decision changes, even if every blocker has since cleared. The same protection is still missing for work prepared outside a transaction and submitted afterwards.

I would keep the scalar decision basis. The following findings are reachable through existing entry points combined with the proposed step-2 operations. The first four can admit stale work or leave an ordinary story with no route forward.

**S3-9.1 · BLOCKING · Reachable through `Stories.assess`, `Stories.seal` and `publish_roadmap`.** An Analyst prepares an assessment on basis 1. A human answer moves the story to basis 2. The story and unchanged contracts are reconfirmed and the list resealed. The delayed assessment now arrives with the same story and list revisions. If Relay stamps it with the basis current at submission, it can open the human gate with an assessment written before the answer. Require each role-authored submission to name the basis and artefact versions the author actually read. Compare them under the gate lock. This applies to the initial roadmap too: the planning caller needs feature context and its basis before any story exists. Test a delayed assessment **arriving** after clearance, as well as an assessment already stored before the answer. [Current assessment inputs](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/stories.py:220) · [Proposed basis checks](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-16-claude-3a-ii-step-2-design.md:36)

**S3-9.2 · BLOCKING · Reachable through `Behaviours.run_green`, `reconfirm` and `Integration.integrate`.** Hold a green run from basis 1. On basis 2 the Verifier reconfirms the unchanged contract and explicitly retains the red, the only accepted evidence then available. Release the old green. Section 5 allows it to finish, but the contract is already current. The green can now open a review cycle and reach integration although nobody retained that green on basis 2. Bind reconfirmation to the exact subject version and evidence IDs, and validate those IDs against Relay’s records. An old-basis result arriving later must either remain audit-only or create a fresh obligation to retain that result before downstream use. Apply the same rule to a late review. A previously authorised intent still settles. [In-flight results and retained evidence](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-16-claude-3a-ii-step-2-design.md:52) · [Green settlement](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/behaviours.py:357)

**S3-9.3 · BLOCKING · Reachable through `ask`, `answer`, `Stories.decide` and `Stories.assess`.** Ask and answer a question while a story is `awaiting_approval`. The gate becomes stale, but the current evaluator skips that story state, resealing requires a story at work, and a second assessment of the unchanged story/list revisions conflicts with the existing assessment. Define one transactional invalidation transition: supersede the old gate and inbox item, keep the story slot, return the story to a state that permits resealing, and key the fresh seal and assessment by decision basis as well as revisions. The old approval must be stale immediately. Test this history through to approval of a *new* manifest after restart. [Gate evaluation](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/stories.py:441) · [Assessment uniqueness](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/persistence/migrations/0007_stories.sql:61)

**S3-9.4 · BLOCKING · Reachable through `publish_roadmap`, `register_acceptance_test` and `reopen_red`.** Section 4 relies on amendment routes that do not exist. A second roadmap publication is refused. Registering a contract moves the behaviour out of the only state that permits registration. Reopening red preserves the old contract. A Verifier who concludes an answer changes the expected result therefore cannot implement that conclusion through the proposed route. Specify a versioned contract amendment that retires the old evidence pairing and requires fresh red. For a stale roadmap, specify who can reaffirm the unchanged revision, whether fresh PO approval is required, and how release treats its prior approval. If changing roadmap content remains step 3, give it an explicit blocked outcome in step 2. **S3-9.5 · BLOCKING · Reachable through `answer`, `Assignments.claim` and `reconfirm`.** Answer a question about a planned story with no behaviours yet. The design owes the Analyst a reconfirmation, but every current assignment requires a behaviour ID. The proposed completion operation also omits the grant, so an Analyst whose grant was revoked by pause could complete the work after resume while the basis remained unchanged. Give the assignment a typed subject and bind its first effective completion to the current grant, subject revision and target basis. Enumerate which artefacts get reconfirmation assignments: stories and contracts have owners for this operation, whereas seals and assessments must be produced afresh. Test the planned story without creating a placeholder behaviour. [Assignment constraints](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/persistence/migrations/0009_assignments.sql:4) · [Proposed completion and dispatch](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-16-claude-3a-ii-step-2-design.md:52)

**S3-9.6 · BLOCKING · Reachable through `ask`, `answer`, `Features.close` and story release.** Opening a question must compose with an existing pause and other open questions. Answering the story question while a feature question remains cannot release work. Resuming a feature cannot clear either question. Cancelling must close outstanding question inbox items and prevent a late first answer from owing new work. Conversely, clearing the *last* blocker must wake a planned story even after restart: the current `Features.reconcile` pass does not call `release_next`; the resume transaction does. Specify the combined eligibility rule and invoke gate evaluation and ordered release when eligibility changes. Test a blocked first planned story, a later story in the same feature, and another eligible feature. The later story must not jump its predecessor. [Release selection](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/stories.py:543) · [Resume transaction](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/stories.py:525) · [Reconciliation pass](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/features.py:53)

**S3-9.7 · BLOCKING · Reachable through the proposed `ask`, `answer` and `replace` operations.** Request receipts alone do not establish one answer per question or one successor per decision. Two different request IDs can target the same open question or the same decision the PO originally read. Require a first answer to target a still-open question and a replacement to target a still-current decision head, under the lock. Preserve project, feature and scope on replacement, and make those relationships database constraints. Otherwise narrowing a feature decision to one story can remove it from a sibling’s context without advancing that sibling’s scalar basis. Give `ask` a stable request identity too: losing its reply and retrying must produce one inbox item. Test competing requests with different IDs as well as exact replay. [Decision and replacement rules](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-16-claude-3a-ii-step-2-design.md:23)

**S3-9.8 · NON-BLOCKING · The proposed test history is unreachable through `publish_roadmap`.** Section 6 asks for a story added by a roadmap revision *while* a feature-wide question is open. Section 1 forbids precisely that publication. Test both commit orders instead. If publication wins, the question stops the newly committed story. If asking wins, publication is refused; after the answer, a submission prepared against the old basis remains stale. That tests scope inheritance and authorisation through real entry points. [Scope rule](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-16-claude-3a-ii-step-2-design.md:19) · [Proposed test](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-16-claude-3a-ii-step-2-design.md:73)

Model assumptions can remain informational. Define gate manifests as immutable snapshots of the decisions and assumptions shown when the gate opened; `context_for` can return the current list. That preserves an exact answer to what the human approved without making each new model assumption reopen a gate.
