# Astra's third review of the slice 3b design (v3)

Provenance: written by Astra (gpt-6.2-astra via Codex) on 2026-11-21 in reply to `round-43-claude-3b-design-v3.md`; copied verbatim below.

**Step 1 can be built once S3-20.1 to S3-20.4 are folded into its contract.** V3 establishes when a returned build is recoverable and who may authorise another paid call. The remaining step 1 gaps concern who can carry out that retry, which oracle try may settle, what happens to a known result during a pause, and lock order. The later findings can wait for their stated build steps.

**S3-20.1 · BLOCKING · Step 1 · Reachable through worker exit and `Invocations.decide(retry)`.** A worker dies with an `intended` call. Recovery parks the assignment, and the person authorises a retry. Section 2 clears the park and re-announces the command, but no transition releases the dead worker’s grant. A replacement worker cannot claim it. The same gap exists if the owner dies after recording `errored` or `rejected` and before admitting the permitted retry. Specify recovery for each of those states. Preserve the original grant and checkout as evidence, make the authorised attempt claimable by a live worker, and keep the retry limit on the assignment occasion across grant changes. Test with two distinct worker IDs. For a review, the successor must prepare its own checkout. [Claim ownership](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/assignments.py:386) · [Retry and recovery rules](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-43-claude-3b-design-v3.md:21)

**S3-20.2 · BLOCKING · Step 1 effect contract, exercised in step 2 · Reachable through loss of the worker’s advisory-lock connection.** Loss of that connection can leave the first oracle running. Recovery marks try 1 interrupted and starts try 2 under the original grant. If try 1 then returns, today’s `finish_green_run` marks it finished and checks only that the grant still holds. Try 1 can therefore open a review cycle even after try 2 was admitted. Require settlement to compare the effect’s current try ID and state in the verdict transaction. An interrupted try may contribute evidence only. Test a late passing try 1 after try 2 starts and fails. No cycle should open. [Green settlement](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/assignments.py:299)

**S3-20.3 · BLOCKING · Step 1 · Reachable through `pause` after Record and before Apply.** A Builder’s candidate is pinned and its invocation is `returned`, but green has not started. Pause can revoke the grant because the existing build first effect is the green run. Section 4 then marks the invocation `overtaken`. After resume, the assignment is still wanted, yet its latest invocation prohibits another call. Conversely, if paid admission is made the first effect and the grant survives, section 4’s listed checks omit pause and open questions before publication. State the rule explicitly. A temporary stop holds a known result and prevents new effects. On clearance, Relay revalidates the recorded basis, base and assignment before continuing. A permanent supersession ends the occasion and independently determines whether fresh work is owed. Test pause after retention, zero publication while paused, and one publication after resume with no second model call. [Grant revocation](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/assignments.py:104) · [Effect admission](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-43-claude-3b-design-v3.md:40)

**S3-20.4 · BLOCKING · Step 1 admission order, exercised in step 2 · Reachable through build admission racing `Behaviours.reopen_red`.** V2 admission locks the assignment. V3 then needs the behaviour lock to allocate its attempt number. `reopen_red` already takes the behaviour lock and then retires the same build assignment. Both hold the project gate shared, so each can wait for the other. Specify behaviour before assignment wherever admission needs both, then re-read the grant under those locks. Race the two entry points. Reopening first must leave zero paid calls for the retired build. [Admission order](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-42-claude-3b-design-v2.md:27) · [`reopen_red`](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/behaviours.py:343)

**S3-20.5 · BLOCKING · Step 2 · Reachable through the real confinement suite and tool-enabled launch.** Section 7 requires every probe to produce a `tool_use` and a refusal. A tool removed from the role’s available set cannot reliably produce that pair, and loading a candidate-local `CLAUDE.md` is not a tool call at all. Give each probe an observable pass condition appropriate to it. For an available tool, require its attempted operation and outcome. For a removed tool, verify the effective tool inventory. For automatic loading, verify the effective loaded context or configuration. Also bind the pass to effective managed policy: Claude can receive that policy through MDM or server-managed settings while the local settings file remains unchanged. D-5 may stay deferred once the boundary actually in force is attestable. I checked the CLI help and documentation without making a paid call. [Claude CLI reference](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/cli-reference) · [Managed settings sources](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/settings)

**S3-20.6 · BLOCKING · Step 3 · Reachable through `plan`, `ask`, `pause`, `answer`, `resume`.** The Analyst asks a question before the first roadmap. The person pauses the feature and answers while it is paused, which `Decisions.answer` permits. If section 1 requires the feature to be open to owe the successor, no successor is created. On resume there is no story for section 9 to evaluate. Record the successor whenever the obligation survives, even while it cannot start. Test one wanted `plan` after the answer, zero calls during pause and exactly one call after resume. For a `review` successor, also bind the existing logical review task to the new assignment so `prepare_review` can accept its grant. [Answer transaction](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/decisions.py:201) · [Review assignment check](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/reviews.py:499)

**S3-20.7 · BLOCKING · Step 3 · Reachable through `specify` overtaken after `proposed`.** Let the proposal commit, then let a human decision overtake the invocation before contract registration. After reconfirmation, the story contains a behaviour with no contract. It qualifies for neither red nor build, and the “every behaviour is on trunk” condition prevents another `specify`. Either commit proposal, entry into test design and contract registration together for this delivered reply, or owe a continuation targeting the existing unfinished behaviour. The recovery test should end with one behaviour identity and one registered contract. [Behaviour transitions](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/domain/behaviour.py:60) · [Derived work table](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/docs/consensus/slice-3/round-43-claude-3b-design-v3.md:101)

**S3-20.8 · BLOCKING · Step 3 · Reachable through `amend_contract` and reconciliation.** Section 9 gives the amended contract’s red to both the reconfirmation Apply and the control-plane pass. The amendment already completes the Verifier’s assignment, so its second effect cannot depend on that grant remaining live. Use one durable red request keyed to the behaviour and red cycle. Registration or amendment owes it in its transaction, and the pass owns execution. Give a definitive invalid red a recorded disposition as well: an observation that already passes leaves an ordinary behaviour awaiting red, so “no red is verified” alone would schedule the same observation on every pass. Test Apply racing the pass, then repeat the pass after an invalid red. [Amendment completion](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/application/decisions.py:615) · [Red verdict transitions](/Users/andrealaforgia/dev/relay-v3/src/relay/domain/behaviour.py:74)

I accept the assignment-successor identity, retaining the candidate before `returned`, the recorded remediation base, and control-plane ownership of generation one. The existing human boundary after a remote push refusal also stands. None of the remaining findings requires provider-transcript recovery, distributed leases or another design round.
