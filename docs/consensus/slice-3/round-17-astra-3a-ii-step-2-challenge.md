**Keep the scalar basis and the scope lookup.** V2 answers S3-9.3, S3-9.7 and S3-9.8. It also gives story gates a viable recovery path and makes roadmap content changes an explicit step-3 correction. The remaining gaps concern which evidence a basis belongs to, who may amend it, and what wakes held work. Each history below uses public operations and requires no infrastructure fault.

**S3-10.1 · BLOCKING · Reachable through `answer`, `reconfirm` and `Behaviours.run_red`.** Section 4 requires only the **story** to be current for `run_red`. Reconfirm the story while its registered contract is still stale, then run red. The run begins on the new basis but executes the old contract. A basis comparison at settlement succeeds, and the Verifier can subsequently be offered that red as evidence to retain. Require a current story **and contract** before launching red. Also fence a red already in flight when `amend_contract` changes the contract on the *same* basis: increment the red cycle on amendment and compare the launch contract revision at settlement. Test both orderings, asserting zero launches in the first and evidence-only settlement in the second. **S3-10.2 · BLOCKING · Reachable through `Assignments.claim`, `Features.close` and `amend_contract`.** Amendment completes the Verifier’s assignment, but its signature takes no grant. Verifier A can prepare an amendment, lose its grant to a pause, then submit after resume and after B claims the assignment. The basis and original contract can still match. Apply the same authority check to both ways of completing this assignment: require its current grant, target basis and expected contract revision. A’s delayed amendment must leave the contract, red cycle and B’s assignment untouched. **S3-10.3 · BLOCKING · Reachable through `Reviews.prepare_review`, `replace` and `Reviews.submit_review`.** A checkout is a first effect, so replacement preserves its grant. The reviewer can prepare again on basis 2 under the same grant. A delayed submission from its basis-1 checkout then presents exactly the same task capability and grant as the fresh submission. Looking up a current checkout by task and grant cannot distinguish them. Require a first submission to identify the exact checkout receipt that produced it and compare that receipt’s basis. Test the old submission **after** the second checkout exists. Preserve exact replay of an already effective submission. **S3-10.4 · BLOCKING · Reachable through `Integration.integrate`, `replace`, `reconfirm` and intent reconciliation.** Hold an authorised push, move to basis 2, and reconfirm the contract, red, green and cycle. There is no completed integration to name yet. Now let the push land. Its settlement must record trunk accurately, but no Verifier has retained that integration on basis 2. Define this ordering explicitly. The simplest rule is to leave contract reconfirmation owed until the outstanding intent settles, then present its actual outcome to the Verifier. Preserve the original execution and authorisation bases when recording retention on a later basis. Test one trunk advance and no story gate before the completed integration is accounted for. **S3-10.5 · BLOCKING · Reachable through `ask`, `answer` and the last `reconfirm`.** Let regression checks finish while a question is open, so the batch becomes `held`. Answer and complete all reconfirmations. Section 6 calls for gate evaluation and ordered story release, but neither hands on a held batch. That happens in the reconciliation pass, which v2 schedules on start and resume. The feature was never manually paused, so it can now sit open indefinitely. Make clearing the last applicable blocker trigger reconciliation of held batches and re-announcement of wanted work, with restart recovery if the process dies after commit. Test one answer, the required reconfirmations and one integration **without** an extra resume or explicit pass. **S3-10.6 · BLOCKING · Reachable through `context_for`, `replace` and any role-authored submission.** The returned decision heads and basis must come from **one database snapshot**. Otherwise a caller can read the old decision text, race with replacement, read the new basis, and submit work that passes every write-side comparison. Specify one query, a consistent snapshot, or the shared gate lock across both reads. The race test should allow only the complete old pair or the complete new pair, including for discovery before a story exists. This finishes the read side of S3-9.1. **S3-10.7 · NON-BLOCKING · Reachable through `Features.close(... cancel)` followed by `replace`.** Cancellation withdraws open questions, but a previously answered decision remains a head. As written, replacing it can create fresh reconfirmation assignments on a permanently cancelled feature. Make a first replacement against a cancelled feature, or a decision scoped to an already approved story, stale with no work owed. A genuine replay still receives its original receipt.
